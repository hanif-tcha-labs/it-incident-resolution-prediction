Association vs Causality: Permutation importance measures how much the model depends on a feature to make accurate statistical forecasts. It never means that changing a field in a ticket will cause the resolution time to change.

Permutation Importance on Test Set

Increase in test MAE (hours) when each feature is individually shuffled (5 repeats)

Feature Contribution Dynamics:
  • assignment_group is the dominant predictor (+14.06 h MAE increase), followed by sys_created_by (+8.74 h) and category (+3.54 h).
  • Priority vs Group: priority, impact, and urgency contribute minimal standalone gain (+0.08 h) once the technical handling team is known. In practice, ticket priority reflects requester escalation rather than technical problem complexity.
  • sys_created_by captures automated ticket routing patterns: system-generated tickets resolve in median 0.4 h versus 48.5 h for manually triaged records.
