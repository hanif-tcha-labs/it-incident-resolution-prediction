Known Limitations

Evaluation results describe this dataset and its historical processes.

  • Long incidents are difficult to estimate. Tickets lasting more than 24 hours make up 36% of the test set and account for 91% of its total forecast error. Their average absolute error is 297.3 hours, compared with 8.2 hours for incidents under one hour.
  • Historical process changes affect accuracy. The dataset's median resolution time fell from about 80 hours to about 2 hours in late March 2016. Results may change when workflows or service policies differ.
  • Some groups are sparsely represented. Critical-priority tickets are less than 1% of the evaluation set, so their estimates are less certain. Several ticket attributes are anonymized.
  • Predictions reflect association, not causation. Changing a ticket field or assignment does not guarantee a change in resolution time.
  • The model covers resolved incidents. The 13 incidents without a resolution target were excluded from training and evaluation.

Recommended Use

  • Review the prediction range alongside the point estimate.
  • Use predictions as one input to prioritization; retain human review for complex or critical incidents.
  • Re-evaluate model performance when incident workflows or category distributions change.
