Temporal Leakage Boundary (Opening vs Lifecycle)

Categorization of attributes by operational availability

PERMITTED AT OPENING (13 Features)

Values extracted strictly from the first logged event:

  • Classification: priority, impact, urgency, category, subcategory, u_symptom
  • Context & People: assignment_group, location, contact_type, opened_by, sys_created_by
  • Temporal: hour (0..23), weekday (0=Mon .. 6=Sun) derived from opened_at

EXCLUDED: LIFECYCLE LEAKAGE (Quarantined)

Fields created or updated after ticket opening:

  • Progress counters: reassignment_count, reopen_count, sys_mod_count
  • Handling metadata: incident_state, active, assigned_to, knowledge, made_sla
  • Closure fields: closed_code, resolved_by, resolved_at, closed_at
  • Linked changes: problem_id, rfc, vendor, caused_by
